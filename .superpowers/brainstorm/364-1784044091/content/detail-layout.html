Recipe detail page: which layout?

This page carries the most content: photo, times/servings, diet badges, ingredients, step-by-step instructions, nutrition, your star rating, and the comment section. Both options are in style B.

A

Full-width hero, single column

Big photo on top, then title + meta, then ingredients, steps, nutrition, and comments flow down one readable column. Classic recipe-blog reading experience; effortless on mobile.
